How to Use
Step 1
Type your lobby name in the "Input text" box. You can use up to 35 characters, and the counter shows how many are left
Step 2
Select the part of the text you want to change with your mouse or finger, or tap Select all to style everything at once
Step 3
Pick your look in the Options panel: Color, Font, B / I / U / S (bold, italic, underline, strikethrough), a Gradient or a Stroke outline
Step 4
Click ✦ Apply to selection. Check the result in the Preview, and use Dark / Light to see how it looks on both backgrounds
Step 5
Happy with it? Click the Output box or the Copy button, then paste it where you set your lobby name
Gradient
Open Gradient ▾ and choose a Start and End color. Tick Middle color to add a third, or press ⇄ to swap the ends. The colors fade letter by letter and spaces are skipped. While Gradient is open, it replaces the single Color
Stroke
Open Stroke ▾ to add an outline. Pick its color and use the Thickness slider (1 to 8). It is applied together with your other options when you click Apply
More tips
Lock Text stops your text from changing while you format. Clear formatting resets the selected part. Made a mistake? Press ↩ Undo. Some fonts, like Highway Gothic and Zekton, look different in the preview than in-game. Press Esc to close this guide
